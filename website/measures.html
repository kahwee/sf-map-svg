About this election snapshot

Certified June 25, 2026. This archive covers the four local measures in the June 2, 2026 election. It does not show live returns or upcoming ballots.

Source: San Francisco Department of Elections, final District Statement of the Vote. Election Day and Vote by Mail are combined; all eleven district totals reconcile to citywide counts. Downloaded September 26, 2026.

Yes share is Yes ÷ (Yes + No); No share uses the same denominator. Undervotes and overvotes are excluded. “vs. city” is the difference in Yes share, in percentage points. A required two-thirds; B–D required more than half of valid citywide votes. Rounded percentages do not determine passage.

Maps reuse the 2022 district display geometry and precise coastline. They illustrate district results, not precinct or neighborhood results. Colors express vote share, not endorsements. District colors preserve the map’s original palette.
