    Sources and method

    Where these numbers come from

    Yes share is Yes divided by Yes plus No; blank and over-marked ballots are excluded. District rows are summed and checked against the certified citywide totals. A measure passes on the citywide vote, using a simple majority or two thirds as the ballot requires, so a district’s share never decides the outcome.
