Source & method

San Francisco Department of Elections, final District Statement of the Vote. Election Day and Vote by Mail totals are combined. District totals reconcile to citywide counts for each measure. Downloaded September 26, 2026.

Measure A required two-thirds of valid votes. Measures B, C and D required a simple majority. Outcomes here are calculated from certified citywide counts and those thresholds. District figures are comparisons, not separate election outcomes.

The map uses the package’s existing 2022 district display geometry and coastline. It is a city-scale illustration, not a precinct map. Election data is bundled separately from the map package; loading this page makes no requests to election services.
