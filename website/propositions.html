    Reading the numbers

    Yes share is Yes votes divided by Yes plus No. Undervotes and overvotes are excluded. District totals were checked against San Francisco’s certified citywide totals. These are votes cast in each supervisorial district, not a portrait of the people who live there, and they do not show statewide outcomes.
