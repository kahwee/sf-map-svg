A few ways to explore

01

Find your neighborhood

Try “NoPa” or “Inner Mission” in the search box, then choose a result to zoom in. Download its GeoJSON to take the boundary with you.

02

Change your perspective

Compare SFAR realtor, SF Find, and city analysis definitions. Switch to Districts mode to see the 2022 supervisorial boundaries.

03

Get a closer look

Use + / − to zoom and drag to pan. On a phone, enable Touch navigation first, then choose Done to scroll again. With the map focused, arrow keys pan and Home resets.

Neighborhood names and boundaries vary by source. The default is the 92 SFAR realtor areas from August 2010; these are not universal or legal boundaries. Parks use actual property boundaries. Read the geographic sources →
